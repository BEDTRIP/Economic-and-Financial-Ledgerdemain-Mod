[h1]E&F: Ledgerdemain [1.13][/h1]
A fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3143591632]Economic and Financial Mod (E&F) - V4[/url] by [b]EBTX[/b] with [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3420714166]Private Sector Construction[/url] built in.
[i]Ledger[/i] — the book of accounts; [i]legerdemain[/i] — sleight of hand. E&F conjured metal and money out of thin air; Ledgerdemain books every move on both sides.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3385002128][1.13] Community Mod Framework[/url]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3508296963][1.13] Expanded Topbar Framework[/url]
[/list]
[b]Do not load E&F, PSC or E&F Hotfix with it[/b] — they are inside.

[h2]What is different from E&F[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Double-entry money.[/b] Central bank reserves, banks, the treasury, businesses and pops are accounts; every flow between them, and between countries, nets out. No metal or money from nowhere.
[*][b]One "bank settlements" good[/b] instead of 57 currency goods. Currencies stay — laws, mints, exchange rates — and the freed goods slots let E&F run next to other big mods.
[*][b]Banks[/b] as buildings owned by E&F's bank companies; deposits, consols, the key rate panel, monetary policy, currency zones for subjects, a world clearing of payments.
[*][b]Construction runs on PSC:[/b] sectors make construction goods, paid by the treasury and the investment pool; households buy construction too.
[*]Dead E&F mechanics removed, not patched over; E&F's error spam cleaned out.
[/list]
