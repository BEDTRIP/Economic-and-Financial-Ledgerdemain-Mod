# the CB (zz_ef_cb_law_sync, monthly) until founding and closing a CB by law (R4а, step 4). The kinds behave apart
# (Д.R4а.5): the CB's profit, its loan to the treasury (zz_ef_cb_loan_cap), the rate rule's weights
# (zz_ef_cb_kind_w_value). The state and the private CB -- with the technology central_banking; none -- always.

	group = lawgroup_ld_central_bank
	icon = "gfx/interface/icons/law_icons/gold_standard.dds"
