# buys services (premises, couriers, guards, lawyers); a bank spends little on goods, mostly on people.
#
# One level: 1000 clerks, no capitalists. Settlement services = liquidity_currency, base
# price 1; paper and services ~30 each. Inputs ~40-45% of the output's value; each era gives more services per
# worker, so the services get cheaper over time.
#   method                 tech                          input                                       output
#   money changer          -- (no technology)            services 4                     (~120)          300
#   counting house         -- (no technology)             paper 2, services 5            (~210)          500
#   joint-stock bank       joint_stock_companies (2)     paper 3, services 8            (~330)          750
#   telegraph transfers    electric_telegraph            paper 3, services 11           (~420)         1000
#   modern bank            modern_financial_instruments  paper 2, services 14, telephones 2, electricity 2  1600
# Stage 2, night 6.10 (Д2.25): every method takes 0.01 gold and 0.02 silver per workforce unit (~1 money each) as the
# base of the banks' metal purchase; the week's quantity is set by a percent modifier (zz_ef_bank_metal_buy, as E&F
# steers its exchanges' inputs): -100% (none) when the reserves are at their norm.
# Demand: ~5% of GDP (Д2.2). !! TUNING !! the outputs, with the businesses' input
# (production_methods/00_ef_market_liquidity.txt) and the seed (script_values/ld_bank_values.txt: GDP / 520000).
#############################################
